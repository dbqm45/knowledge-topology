# Performance Reviews

## Cycle

Performance reviews take place twice per year. Employees write a self-assessment, managers collect peer
feedback and hold a review conversation.

## Calibration

People Operations runs calibration sessions with managers to keep ratings consistent across teams. The
review ratings feed into the salary review process.
