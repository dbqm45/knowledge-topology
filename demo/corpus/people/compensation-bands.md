# Compensation Bands

## Bands

Every role is mapped to a compensation band. Salary reviews happen once per year after the performance
review cycle. Band ranges are confidential and visible only to People Operations and managers.

## Salary review process

Managers propose salary changes within the band. People Operations validates the proposals against the
budget and the band ranges.
