# Employee Onboarding

## Before the first day

People Operations prepares the employment contract, the equipment order and the accounts. The hiring
manager assigns an onboarding buddy.

## First week

New employees complete the security training, meet their buddy and receive their onboarding plan for the
first 90 days.

## Probation

The probation period is 3 months and ends with a probation review with the manager.
