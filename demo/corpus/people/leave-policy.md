# Leave Policy

## Annual leave

Employees receive 26 days of annual leave per year. Unused leave can be carried over until the end of March.

## Sick leave

Sick leave must be reported to the manager and to People Operations on the first day of absence.

## Parental leave

Parental leave follows the statutory rules; the company tops up parental pay to 100 percent for 12 weeks.
